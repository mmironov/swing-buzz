Patrik & Lizette

Patrik and Lizette have been dance partners since 2015 when they started dancing with the internationally known dance company The Hot Shots. They are based in Stockholm and have specialized in Lindy Hop and other swing dances. On stage they perform with both character and feeling. Together, they travel the world to perform and teach. Lizette and Patrik draw inspiration from original dancers, like the legendary performers in Whitey's Lindy Hoppers, and place great value on the roots of dance, while always keeping personal style in focus.

Lizette is coming back to Swing Buzz Festival
but this will be Patrik's first
appearance at the event.
