Concept

The festival for Old School Jazz and Lindy Hop

At Swing Buzz Festival you will emerse yourself in the world of Lindy Hop and Jazz done in the old school way.


Get to experience:

  • Dance classes with top international teachers that draw their inspiration from the old clips.
  • History talks with the legendary Lennart Westerlund.
  • Late night parties with a LIVE band and experienced DJs that bring the classic swinging tunes.
  • Sharing and having fund with passionate dancers from all around the world.

Levels you can choose from:

  • Intermediate - you have been dancing Lindy Hop for at least 1 year
  • Advanced - you have significant experience in Lindy Hop and you can't wait to improve
  • Pro - fixed partner track. You are a performer, teacher, competitor and are serious about your dancing. The material in this level will cover faster tempos, air steps and other more advanced aspects of the dance.

Schedule

Schedule

The festival will start with the first party on Friday (3 Oct) around 21:00 and will end late during the night with the last party on Sunday (5 Oct).

Classes will be held on Saturday and Sunday with the first class starting at 10:00 on Saturday and the last class ending before 18:00 on Sunday.

The exact schedule of the festival will be announced in September.

All locations are in the Sofia city center on walking distance from each other.
