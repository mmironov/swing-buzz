Concept

The festival for Old School Jazz and Lindy Hop

At Swing Buzz Festival you will immerse yourself in the world of Lindy Hop and Jazz done in the old school way.


Get to experience:

  • Dance classes with top international teachers that draw their inspiration from the old clips.
  • Talks covering historical aspects of the dance
  • Late night parties with the hottest LIVE bands and experienced DJs that bring the classic swinging tunes.
  • Sharing and having fun with passionate dancers from all around the world.

Levels and tracks you can choose from:

  • Intermediate You have been dancing Lindy Hop for at least 1 year
  • Advanced You have significant experience in Lindy Hop and you can't wait to improve
  • Pro Fixed partner track. You are a performer, teacher, competitor and are serious about your dancing. The material in this level will cover faster tempos, air steps and other more advanced aspects of the dance.
  • NEW Jazz Performance Track Work on a Jazz routine with Lizette and perform it at the Saturday party.
